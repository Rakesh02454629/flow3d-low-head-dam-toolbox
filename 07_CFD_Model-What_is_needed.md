<h1> CFD Model What is needed? </h1>



<p>

The Following data are required for carrying out CFD modeling:

</p>



<ul>

&#x20; <li> High resolution Digital elevation m (Terrain model).</li>

&#x20; <li> Bathymetric data: Upstream water-surface and Downstream tailwater elevation, Flow velocity and discharge and channel bed level </li>

&#x20; <li>Flow depth at selected cross sections.</li>

&#x20; <li>Velocity measurements.</li>

&#x20; <li>Visual observations of roller location and free-surface behavior.</li>

&#x20; <li> Low head dam drawing (3D solid geometry) with all appurtenances .</li>

&#x20; <li> USGS discharge time series data for simulating different range of probable flow.</li>

&#x20; <li>Laboratory-scale results, when applicable.</li>

</ul>
