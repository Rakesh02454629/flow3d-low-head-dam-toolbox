<h1> CFD Model What is needed? </h1>



<p>

The Following data are required for carrying out CFD modeling:

</p>



<ul>

&#x20; <li>Upstream water-surface elevation.</li>

&#x20; <li>Downstream tailwater elevation.</li>

&#x20; <li>Flow depth at selected cross sections.</li>

&#x20; <li>Velocity measurements.</li>

&#x20; <li>Visual observations of roller location and free-surface behavior.</li>

&#x20; <li>Laboratory-scale results, when applicable.</li>

</ul>
