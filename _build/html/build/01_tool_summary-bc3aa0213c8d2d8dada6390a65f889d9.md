<h1>Tool Summary</h1>

<h2>Objective and Scope</h2>

<p>
This toolbox demonstrates how FLOW-3D can be used to set up, run, and interpret a three-dimensional CFD model for low-head dam reverse-roller and drowning-potential assessment.
</p>

<p>
This manual toolbox discusses the step-by-step process for simulating flow at low-head dams under different scenarios. It includes analysis of different discharge and head ranges for which a reverse roller forms, along with drowning potential, flow characteristics, discharge, water-surface profile, and velocity distribution.
</p>

<p>
The current example focuses on:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Low-head dam hydraulic hazard analysis</li>
  <li>Free-surface flow simulation</li>
  <li>Velocity and water-surface analysis</li>
  <li>Reverse-roller and submerged-jump behavior</li>
  <li>Identification of high-velocity zones, recirculation zones, and hydraulic conditions that may contribute to public-safety risk</li>
</ul>

<h2>Motivation</h2>

<p>
Three-dimensional CFD modeling can help analyze reverse-roller hydraulics and evaluate potential mitigation alternatives at low-head dams.
</p>

<p>
FLOW-3D outputs also provide visual results that can help communicate hydraulic hazards to dam owners, first responders, government officials, and the public.
</p>

<h2>What This Toolbox Does</h2>

<p>
This toolbox helps users learn how to:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Prepare geometry and terrain or bathymetry data</li>
  <li>Define the computational domain and mesh blocks</li>
  <li>Set fluid properties and initial conditions</li>
  <li>Apply inflow, outflow, wall, and symmetry boundary conditions</li>
  <li>Run a FLOW-3D simulation</li>
  <li>Extract results such as velocity, water-surface elevation, pressure, turbulence, and hydraulic-roller behavior</li>
  <li>Prepare figures, tables, and animations for reporting</li>
</ul>
