<h2>6. Summary of Results</h2>

<p>
The CFD Toolbox for Low-Head Dam Hazard Analysis enables users to generate, analyze, and interpret hydraulic simulation results to identify hazardous flow conditions, particularly reverse rollers and submerged hydraulic jumps. These results support hydraulic hazard assessment, dam safety evaluation, and the development of appropriate mitigation measures.
</p>

<h3>Main Hydraulic Results</h3>

<p>
The primary hydraulic results generated using FLOW-3D include:
</p>

| Result | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Water-surface elevation | Evaluate flow depths, free-surface profiles, and upstream and downstream water levels. |
| Velocity magnitude | Identify high-velocity regions, flow acceleration, and hydraulic conditions near the dam. |
| Pressure distribution | Evaluate pressure variations and hydrodynamic loading on hydraulic structures. |
| Turbulence and vorticity | Identify turbulent flow structures, recirculation zones, and energy dissipation. |
| Reverse roller characteristics | Determine roller formation, location, length, and recirculation intensity. |
| Flow profiles | Compare hydraulic conditions upstream, over the crest, near the dam toe, and downstream. |
| Streamlines and pathlines | Visualize flow direction, circulation patterns, and reverse-flow regions. |
| Flow animations | Illustrate transient flow behavior and communicate hydraulic hazards. |

<h3>Expected Products</h3>

<p>
The toolbox guides users in developing the following outputs:
</p>

<ul>
<li>Plan-view velocity distribution maps.</li>
<li>Longitudinal and transverse velocity profiles.</li>
<li>Water-surface elevation profiles.</li>
<li>Three-dimensional free-surface visualizations.</li>
<li>Reverse roller formation and recirculation zone visualizations.</li>
<li>Pressure distribution and turbulence contour plots.</li>
<li>Comparison of hydraulic results under different discharge and tailwater conditions.</li>
<li>Summary tables of critical hydraulic parameters.</li>
<li>Animations illustrating flow patterns and potential drowning hazards.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Interpretation of Results</h3>

<p>
Users should evaluate the simulation results to determine the reliability of the predicted hydraulic conditions and assess potential safety hazards. Key considerations include:
</p>

<ul>
<li>Whether the simulated flow patterns are physically reasonable.</li>
<li>Whether the simulation has reached steady or quasi-steady conditions, where applicable.</li>
<li>Whether mass conservation and numerical convergence are satisfactory.</li>
<li>Whether mesh resolution adequately captures the hydraulic jump and recirculation regions.</li>
<li>Whether simulated water levels and velocities agree with available laboratory or field measurements.</li>
<li>Whether a reverse roller forms downstream of the dam and how its characteristics change with discharge and tailwater elevation.</li>
<li>Whether the strength and extent of recirculation and upstream-directed surface flow indicate potentially hazardous conditions.</li>
<li>Whether proposed structural modifications or mitigation measures reduce hazardous hydraulic behavior.</li>
</ul>
